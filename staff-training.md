# 👨‍🏫 Staff Emergency Response Training

## 1. Training Objective

The purpose of this training is to teach Internee.pk staff how to recognize, report, and respond to cybersecurity incidents safely and quickly.

Employees are an important part of the organization's incident response process.

---

## 2. Recognizing Warning Signs

Employees should report unusual activity such as:

- Unexpected file-access errors
- Suspicious email attachments
- Suspicious links
- Unexpected login notifications
- Unusual pop-ups
- Unexpected password changes
- Security warnings
- Files suddenly becoming inaccessible
- Unusual computer behavior

---

## 3. What Employees Should Do

If a cybersecurity incident is suspected:

### Step 1 — Stop

Stop interacting with suspicious files, links, or applications.

### Step 2 — Report

Immediately report the incident using the organization's approved security channel.

### Step 3 — Provide Information

Tell the IT/Security Team:

- Your name
- Device name or location
- Approximate time the problem started
- What you observed
- Any suspicious email or message involved

### Step 4 — Follow Instructions

Follow instructions provided by the IT/Security Team.

### Step 5 — Protect Evidence

Do not delete suspicious messages, files, or other potential evidence unless instructed by authorized personnel.

---

# 4. What Employees Should NOT Do

Employees should never:

- Run unknown software.
- Open suspicious attachments again.
- Click suspicious links.
- Delete potential evidence.
- Attempt to investigate malware themselves.
- Share incident information publicly.
- Post incident details on social media.
- Forward suspicious files to coworkers.
- Spread unverified information.
- Communicate with suspected attackers on their own.

---

# 5. Emergency Communication

Example internal security message:

> **SECURITY ALERT:** A potential cybersecurity incident is under investigation. Please do not open suspicious files or links and follow instructions from the IT/Security Team. Further updates will be provided through the approved emergency communication channel.

---

# 6. Ransomware Response Training

Employees receive the following fictional scenario:

> Several documents on a workstation appear to be inaccessible, and a security alert has been generated.

Employees must:

1. Stop normal activity.
2. Report the incident.
3. Provide workstation information.
4. Follow isolation instructions.
5. Avoid interacting with suspicious files.
6. Wait for instructions from the IT/Security Team.

---

# 7. Training Scenario

### Situation

An employee receives an unexpected email containing an attachment.

After opening it, several documents appear to be inaccessible.

### Employee Response

The employee should:

- Stop interacting with the files.
- Report the incident immediately.
- Provide details to IT/Security.
- Follow instructions.
- Avoid spreading the message to other employees.

### Security Team Response

The security team should:

- Assess the report.
- Identify the affected system.
- Isolate the system when appropriate.
- Preserve evidence.
- Check for additional affected systems.
- Escalate according to the incident response plan.

---

| Skill | Pass Criteria |
|---|---|
| Recognizes suspicious behavior | Reports promptly |
| Reports incident | Uses approved channel |
| Protects evidence | Does not delete or alter evidence |
| Follows instructions | Performs only approved actions |
| Communication | Shares verified information only |
| Emergency response | Escalates quickly |

---

# 9. Training Schedule

### New Employees

Cybersecurity awareness training should be provided during onboarding.

### Existing Employees

Regular refresher training should be conducted.

### IT/Security Staff

Technical incident-response exercises should be conducted regularly.

### After an Incident

Additional targeted training should be provided based on lessons learned.

---

# 10. Training Checklist

- [ ] Employee understands how to report an incident.
- [ ] Employee knows the emergency contact/channel.
- [ ] Employee can recognize common warning signs.
- [ ] Employee knows what not to do.
- [ ] Employee understands evidence preservation.
- [ ] Employee understands ransomware response procedures.
- [ ] Employee knows how to follow IT/Security instructions.

---

# 11. Expected Result

After completing the training, employees should be able to:

**Recognize → Stop → Report → Follow Instructions → Protect Evidence**

This helps reduce the impact of cybersecurity incidents and improves the organization's overall security readiness.
